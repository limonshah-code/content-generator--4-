## What to Look for Before Buying the Best American Standard Cadet 3 Toilets

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, determine the rough-in size of your bathroom, which is usually 12 inches from the wall to the bolts. You should also decide between an elongated bowl, which offers more comfort, or a round bowl, which saves space in tight quarters. If you have elderly family members or individuals with limited mobility, a Right Height or chair-height bowl is highly recommended for safety and ease of use.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($250-$600 ranges)
Cadet 3 toilets generally fall into the mid-range price bracket. Basic two-piece models can start around $250, while specialized one-piece or skirted models can go up to $500 or $600. Keep in mind that one-piece models often cost more due to the complexity of manufacturing but save you money on potential leak repairs and cleaning time later.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for the WaterSense label if you want to save on your utility bills. A 3-inch flush valve is a hallmark of the Cadet 3 series and is crucial for providing that powerful flush. The trapway size also matters; a larger, fully glazed trapway reduces the friction of waste and significantly lowers the chance of clogs.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Vitreous china is the standard for high-quality toilets. You should check if the model features the EverClean surface, which is an antimicrobial glaze that stays cleaner longer. For internal parts, models with metal trip levers and metal fill valve shanks generally last much longer than those with plastic parts.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
American Standard is one of the most trusted names in plumbing. Choosing an established brand ensures that you will have access to replacement parts, such as flappers or tank lids, for years to come. Their warranties are generally industry-standard, offering peace of mind for your investment.

### Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
If you are installing the toilet yourself, consider the weight. One-piece toilets are significantly heavier and may require two people to position. For small bathrooms, look specifically for compact elongated models, which provide the comfort of an elongated seat while fitting into the footprint of a round-front toilet.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy american standard cadet 3 toilets?

Investing in an American Standard Cadet 3 toilet is an excellent decision for most homeowners. These toilets are specifically designed to bridge the gap between expensive luxury fixtures and unreliable budget options. The primary advantage of this line is the Cadet 3 flushing system, which uses a larger valve and trapway than standard toilets. This design translates to fewer clogs and a cleaner bowl with every use.

Compared to older toilets that use 3.5 or even 5 gallons per flush, the 1.28 gpf Cadet 3 models can save a family of four thousands of gallons of water per year. This not only helps the environment but pays for the toilet itself over time through lower water bills. Furthermore, the inclusion of features like the EverClean surface and slow-close seats as standard in many models adds a level of modern convenience that older fixtures lack. While the initial cost may be slightly higher than a generic big-box store model, the long-term benefits of durability and reduced maintenance make it a much wiser investment.
