## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Underwater Housings Under 100

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before purchasing, verify that the housing is explicitly designed for your device. Universal smartphone housings are great for occasional use, but dedicated housings like those for GoPro or Olympus TG-series offer better ergonomics and access to specific controls. Consider whether you are snorkeling (shallow) or scuba diving (deep), as this determines the necessary depth rating.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($20-$100 ranges)
Most housings in this category fall between $25 and $95. The lower end typically offers basic PMMA plastic shells for action cameras, while the upper end provides features like Bluetooth control, vacuum seals, or aluminum construction. Investing slightly more usually gets you better lens clarity and more durable hardware.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for depth ratings that exceed your planned activities by at least 10 meters for safety. Optical clarity is vital; look for housings with 'tempered glass' or 'multi-coated' lenses to ensure your 4K video isn't blurred. If you are using a smartphone, decide if you need touchscreen access or if physical Bluetooth buttons are preferred.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Saltwater is incredibly corrosive. Ensure the housing uses stainless steel for all buttons and hinges. The quality of the O-ring (the rubber seal) is the most critical component. Higher-quality housings often include a spare O-ring or special tools for cleaning and greasing the seal to prevent leaks.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Brands like FitStill, SeaFrogs, and OM SYSTEM have established histories in underwater photography. Choosing a recognized brand often means better customer support, more reliable depth testing, and a higher standard of quality control compared to generic, no-name alternatives.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Underwater housings can significantly increase the bulk of your camera. If you are a light traveler, look for slim-profile designs. However, remember that larger housings often have better buoyancy and are easier to hold steady under water. Check if the housing includes a wrist strap or a mounting point for a tray or light.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy underwater housings under 100?

Investing in an underwater housing under 100 is an excellent decision for recreational divers and travelers. These products allow you to leverage the high-quality camera you already own—whether it is an iPhone or a GoPro—to capture memories that would otherwise be impossible to document. Compared to expensive professional housings that cost thousands, these budget-friendly options provide about 90 percent of the same functionality for a fraction of the price.

Modern engineering has brought professional features like vacuum ports and aluminum shells down to consumer price levels. While you might sacrifice some advanced control or extreme depth ratings (over 100 meters), the value proposition is unbeatable for the average user. Long-term benefits include saving money on renting gear and the ability to share high-quality content on social media immediately after your dive.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose underwater housings under 100?

Choosing the right housing starts with an honest assessment of your diving depth. If you never go below 10 meters while snorkeling, a simple PMMA shell is perfect. For scuba divers, an 80-meter or 60-meter rating provides a necessary safety margin.

A common mistake is forgetting about lighting. Water absorbs red light very quickly as you descend, making your footage look blue or green. Choose a housing that either supports red filters or has a mounting point for an external video light like the LetonPower L12. Always perform a 'dry test' by submerging the empty housing in a sink or tub before putting your expensive electronics inside. If you see bubbles, the seal is compromised. Finally, consider if you want to use your phone's touchscreen or if you prefer tactile buttons; at greater depths, touchscreen responsiveness often decreases due to pressure.

**How do I maintain my underwater housings under 100?**
Rinse your housing in fresh water after every saltwater use to prevent salt crystals from damaging the seals. Regularly inspect the O-ring for hair, sand, or cracks, and apply a small amount of silicone grease to keep it supple and watertight.
