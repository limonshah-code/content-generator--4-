## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Campbell Hausfeld Air Tools

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and Use Case Considerations
Before purchasing, evaluate your air compressor's specifications. Air tools are rated by CFM (Cubic Feet per Minute) and PSI (Pounds per Square Inch). If your tool requires 5.0 CFM and your compressor only provides 3.0, the tool will not function correctly. Match high-consumption tools like sanders or drills with large-tank compressors. For simple inflation or cleaning, a small portable unit is usually sufficient.

### Budget - Setting Realistic Price Expectations
Basic inflation kits and accessory sets typically range from $15 to $40. Moving up to specialized individual tools like air drills or cut-off tools, you can expect to spend $40 to $80. For comprehensive master kits containing multiple power tools and dozens of accessories, budgets between $100 and $250 are standard. Remember that investing in a quality air dryer for $60 can save you hundreds in tool replacement costs later.
