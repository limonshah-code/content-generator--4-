## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Toolbasix Keyed Door Knobs

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, you must determine if the knob is intended for an interior or exterior door. Exterior doors require a keyed entry for security, while interior doors like bathrooms might only need a privacy lock. Ensure the knob you choose matches the existing holes in your door; most standard doors have a backset of either 2-3/8 inches or 2-3/4 inches. Adjustable latches are a great feature because they eliminate the guesswork and fit both sizes effortlessly.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($15-$40 ranges)
Setting a realistic budget is key to finding the right balance of security and value. Standard residential keyed knobs typically fall between $15 and $25 per unit. If you are looking for advanced features like SmartKey technology or antimicrobial finishes, expect to pay between $30 and $45. Buying multi-packs can often lower the price per unit significantly if you are updating an entire home.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for features like the security grade; Grade 3 is standard for residential use, while Grade 2 or 1 offers higher security for commercial applications. Consider the type of keyway; KW1 or SC1 are common and easy to duplicate. Additionally, look for knobs that include at least three keys if you have a large family, as this saves you the cost and time of getting extra copies made.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Always prioritize all-metal construction over units with plastic internal components. Metal internals are less likely to snap or strip over time, especially in high-traffic areas like the front door or kitchen. The finish also matters; electroplated or PVD finishes are far more resistant to scratching and tarnishing than simple painted surfaces. A heavy feel in the hand usually indicates a higher quality build.

### Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Choosing a brand like Kwikset, BESTTEN, or Copper Creek ensures that you have access to customer support and warranties if something goes wrong.
