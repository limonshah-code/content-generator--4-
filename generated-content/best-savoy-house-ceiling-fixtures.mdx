## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Savoy House Ceiling Fixtures

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase a new light, look at where it will live. If you are lighting a bathroom or outdoor porch, you must choose a wet-rated or damp-rated fixture like the LED disc lights to ensure safety and longevity. For living rooms and bedrooms, a dry-rated fixture like a fabric semi-flush mount is perfectly fine and often offers more style options.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($50-$500)
Savoy House offers a wide range of prices to fit almost any project. You can find high-quality utility LED discs for around $50 to $100. For mid-range designer flush mounts, expect to spend between $150 and $300. If you are looking for a large statement piece like a multi-light semi-flush or a large pendant, the price can range from $300 to $500 or more.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
