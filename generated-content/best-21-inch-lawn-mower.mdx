## What to Look for Before Buying the Best 21 Inch Lawn Mower

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, you must evaluate the size and topography of your yard. If you have a flat, quarter-acre lot, a standard push mower might be sufficient. However, for properties with significant hills or thick, stubborn grass types like St. Augustine, a self-propelled model with a high-torque engine is much more practical.
