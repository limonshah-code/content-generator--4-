## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Long Tube Headers For 5 7 Hemi Ram

### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before you purchase, you must verify the specific year and drive type of your Ram. Most long tube headers are designed for 2WD models, and 4WD trucks often require specific clearance for the front driveshaft. Consider if you want shorty headers for an easy bolt-on experience or long tubes for maximum racing performance.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($300-$1,200 ranges)
Header prices vary wildly based on material and brand. Entry-level stainless options typically range from $300 to $500, offering good value and decent fitment. Premium brands that offer lifetime warranties and patented collector designs can reach $1,200 or more. Factor in the cost of gaskets, O2 extensions, and potential professional installation.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Look for mandrel-bent tubing, which ensures a consistent diameter throughout the curves to prevent exhaust restriction. A thick head flange (at least 3/8 inch) is vital for the 5.7 Hemi to prevent the warping that leads to the common Hemi tick exhaust leak.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
304 stainless steel is the gold standard for headers as it resists rust and high-heat oxidation better than 409 stainless or aluminized steel. Check for Tig welding at the joints, as this provides a stronger bond that can withstand the vibrations and thermal expansion of a high-performance V8 engine.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Buying from established names like JBA or Kooks ensures that the R&D has been done specifically for your vehicle. Trusted brands usually provide better customer support and more accurate fitment guides, which is crucial when you are working in the tight engine bay of a modern truck.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Long tube headers are physically large and require significant space for installation. You may need to relocate steering shafts or remove inner fender liners to get them into place. Ensure you have the necessary tools, such as various extensions and swivel sockets, to reach the mounting bolts in cramped spaces.

## Is it A Wise Decision to Buy long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram?

Investing in long tube headers for your 5.7 Hemi Ram is one of the most impactful decisions you can make for your vehicle. The factory manifolds are notorious for failing, with bolts snapping and flanges warping, leading to the dreaded Hemi tick. By upgrading to high-quality stainless steel headers, you are not just adding power; you are solving a known reliability issue with a permanent fix.

Compared to other modifications like cold air intakes or cat-back exhausts, headers offer the most significant increase in actual horsepower and torque because they address the primary restriction in the engine's breathing cycle. Long-term benefits include improved fuel efficiency during highway cruising and a much more rewarding driving experience. While the initial cost and installation effort are higher than other mods, the results in terms of sound, performance, and durability make it a highly wise investment for any serious truck owner.

## What Is the Best Way to Choose long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram?

Selecting the right headers involves a step-by-step process to ensure you get the best results for your specific truck. First, determine your primary goal: are you looking for a simple fix for a manifold leak, or are you building a high-performance machine? For a daily driver, shorty headers like those from Knetegd or JBA are often the best choice because they bolt directly to your factory mid-pipe.

If you want maximum power, long tubes are the way to go, but remember you will also need O2 extensions and likely a custom tune to keep the truck running perfectly. Avoid the common mistake of buying the cheapest headers available without checking flange thickness; thin flanges will warp just like the factory ones. Always look for 304 stainless steel if you live in an area where roads are salted in the winter. Finally, read user reviews specifically regarding fitment for your specific model year, as small changes in truck design can make a big difference in how easily the headers install.

## FAQ

**What is the best long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram for beginners?**
For beginners, we recommend starting with high-quality shorty headers. They are much easier to install than long tubes because they bolt directly into the factory exhaust location. They provide a significant upgrade in durability over the stock manifolds and solve the manifold leak issue without requiring custom tuning or extensive exhaust work.

**How much should I spend on long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram?**
A budget of $400 to $800 is ideal for a high-quality set of headers. While you can find cheaper options, spending a bit more ensures you get 304 stainless steel and thick flanges. This investment pays off in the long run by preventing future leaks and ensuring the headers last for the life of the truck.

**What features are most important in long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram?**
The two most critical features are the material and the flange thickness. You want 304 stainless steel to prevent rust and a 3/8-inch thick flange to prevent warping. Mandrel-bent tubing is also essential for maintaining maximum exhaust flow and achieving the performance gains you expect from the upgrade.

**How do I maintain my long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram?**
Stainless steel headers require very little maintenance. It is a good practice to re-torque the header bolts after the first few heat cycles to ensure they remain tight. If you have polished headers, you can occasionally clean them with a dedicated stainless steel polish to maintain their shine and remove road grime.

**Are expensive long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram worth it?**
Yes, expensive headers are often worth it because they use higher-grade materials and more advanced collector designs. Brands like JBA and Kooks invest heavily in research to ensure their headers provide the best possible flow and the most precise fitment, which can save you hours of labor and frustration during installation.

**What brands make the best long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram?**
JBA, Knetegd, and Kooks are among the top brands for the 5.7 Hemi Ram. JBA is known for their patented collector technology, while Knetegd offers excellent value with heavy-duty construction. Kooks is widely regarded as a premium choice for high-performance and racing applications.

**How long do long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram typically last?**
A high-quality set of 304 stainless steel headers can easily last for the entire lifespan of your truck. Unlike factory cast-iron manifolds that can crack or warp, stainless steel is designed to handle the extreme heat cycles of a V8 engine without degrading or rusting through.

**Can I find good long tube headers for 5 7 hemi ram on a budget?**
Yes, there are excellent budget-friendly options that do not compromise on the essentials. Look for brands that offer 304 stainless steel and thick flanges even at a lower price point. While they may lack a high-polish finish or complex collector designs, they will still provide a massive improvement over stock.
