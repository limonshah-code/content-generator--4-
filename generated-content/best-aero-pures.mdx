## What to Look for Before Buying the Best Aero Pures

### Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, assess the size of your bathroom and how it is used. For a small powder room, a 50 or 80 CFM fan is usually sufficient. However, for a master bathroom with a large walk-in shower, you should aim for 110 CFM to ensure moisture is removed effectively. Consider if you need integrated lighting or a heater, as these require specific wiring and ceiling space. If you are retrofitting an old fan, measure your joist depth to see if you need a Slim Fit model.

### Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($100-$350 ranges)
Aero Pure products typically range from $100 for basic ventilation fans to over $350 for high-end heater and light combinations. Basic quiet fans with LED lights usually sit in the $150 to $200 range. While higher-end models require a larger upfront investment, their Energy Star certification and long warranties often lead to lower operating costs and fewer replacement needs over time.

### Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Focus on the Sone rating and CFM. A Sone rating of 1.0 or lower is considered quiet, while 0.3 to 0.5 is virtually silent. For CFM, the general rule is 1 CFM per square foot of bathroom space. Look for additional features like nightlights, which are great for safety, and adjustable CFM switches that allow you to fine-tune the performance of the motor based on your room's specific airflow requirements.

### Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Look for fans with galvanized steel housings and high-quality motors engineered for continuous run.
