### 1. Find Your Need - Compatibility and use case considerations
Before making a purchase, identify where your hair problem is most prevalent. If you have mostly high-pile carpets, you need a vacuum with a motorized brush roll and high kPa suction. If you are cleaning mostly hardwood, look for a model with LED headlights and soft-bristled brushes that won't scratch the surface. Multi-functional models that convert to handhelds are best for those who also need to clean stairs and furniture.

### 2. Budget - Setting realistic price expectations ($100-$400 ranges)
Budget stick vacuums typically range from $100 to $180 and offer decent suction for smaller apartments. Mid-range models between $180 and $300 often include better anti-tangle technology and larger batteries. Premium models over $300 usually feature smart sensors, massive suction power (up to 60kPa), and established brand warranties.

### 3. Key Features - Most important technical specifications
Focus on the suction power (measured in kPa) and the motor wattage (W). For long human hair, an anti-tangle brush roll with a V-shape or internal comb is vital. Battery runtime is another key factor; look for at least 40 minutes if you have a medium-sized home. HEPA filtration is a must-have for those sensitive to dust and hair-borne allergens.

### 4. Quality & Durability - What to look for in construction
Check for a brushless motor, as these tend to last longer and run more efficiently than brushed versions. The quality of the plastic joints and the ease of clicking attachments into place can tell you a lot about the build quality. Look for reviews that mention the longevity of the battery over several months of use.

### 5. Brand Reputation - Importance of established manufacturers
Established brands like Shark and Bissell offer excellent customer support and easy access to replacement filters or batteries. However, newer specialized brands like Myteaworld or SMOTURE often provide higher technical specs (like 60kPa suction) at a lower price point. Balance your need for a name brand with your desire for raw power.

### 6. Portability/Size - Considerations for space and storage
Cordless vacuums are meant to be light, but some can feel top-heavy during extended use. If you have limited storage, a self-standing model or one with a small wall-mounted dock is ideal. Check the weight of the handheld portion specifically if you plan on doing a lot of above-floor cleaning like curtains or ceilings.

Choosing the right vacuum involves a step-by-step assessment of your living environment.
